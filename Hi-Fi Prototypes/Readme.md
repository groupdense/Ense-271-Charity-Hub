- **🏠 Home Page** – A welcoming introduction and a gateway to all key resources.  
- **📄 Grant Writing Info** – Essential guidelines for securing funding and financial assistance.  
- **📅 Workshop Events** – A calendar of upcoming charity-focused events and training.  
- **🔗 Resource Hub** – A collection of tools, guides, and networking opportunities tailored for nonprofit organizations.
